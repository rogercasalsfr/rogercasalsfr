### Roger Casals-Franch

Computational biologist and PhD candidate in Bioinformatics at UVic-UCC (IRIS-CC), also working with
IrsiCaixa on therapeutic HIV vaccination. I build mechanistic and predictive models of how immune cells
change state from single-cell and multimodal data: gene regulatory networks, trajectory inference and
perturbation modelling.

[Website](https://rogercasalsfr.github.io/cv/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=EPEU-ioAAAAJ) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0009-0889-6116) · [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/roger-casals-franch-06583282) · [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/rogercasalsf.bsky.social)

**Selected work**

- **T-cell reprogramming under PD-1 blockade**: trajectory inference and GRN analysis of tumour-infiltrating T cells
  ([preprint](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.10.15.682506), [code](https://github.com/rogercasalsfr/Itx_anl), [protocol](https://github.com/rogercasalsfr/immuno-trajectory-grn-integrative-workflow))
- **GRETA**: benchmark of GRN inference from multimodal single-cell data, with the Saez-Rodriguez group
  ([preprint](https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.12.20.629764), [code](https://github.com/saezlab/greta))
- **scHLA-demux**: Snakemake workflow for HLA-based donor demultiplexing of pooled 10x data ([code](https://github.com/rogercasalsfr/scHLA-demux))
- **Open Problems – Single-Cell Perturbations**: top 10% on Kaggle with GRN-informed models ([code](https://github.com/rogercasalsfr/OpenProblems))
- **Boolyne** *(in development)*: Boolean GRNs that reproduce observed cell states and transitions
